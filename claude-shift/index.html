The Problem

Your $200/month subscription
is fighting your schedule.

Claude Max runs on 5-hour usage windows. The moment you send a message, a 5-hour timer starts. When it expires, you get a fresh window. Simple enough — until you realize you don’t control when the timer starts. A casual question at 7am means your window expires at noon — right in the middle of deep work.

How It Works

Set your schedule.
Forget the rest.

Tell claude-shift when you work. It calculates when to fire pings so fresh windows line up with your work blocks. Your OS runs them automatically.

Step 1

Define Your Work Hours

Provide your work schedule — morning coding blocks, evening sessions, whatever your routine looks like.

$ claude-shift smart --slots "09:00-14:00,20:00-23:00"
Step 2

It Calculates Optimal Pings

claude-shift figures out when to fire pings so that fresh 5-hour windows align exactly with your work blocks.

Ping at 04:00 → fresh at 09:00
Ping at 17:00 → fresh at 22:00
Step 3

OS Runs Them Automatically

One command registers everything with your OS scheduler. No daemon, no background process — just your OS doing its job.

$ claude-shift install
✓ Registered with Task Scheduler
claude-shift week
Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don’t.

Smart Mode

Tell it your work hours. It calculates optimal ping times automatically. Multiple work blocks? One ping might cover several — it does the math.

Weekly Timeline

Visualize your entire schedule at a glance. See ping times, slot coverage, and work windows across your whole week in one beautiful view.

Cross-Platform

Native OS scheduler integration everywhere. Windows Task Scheduler, macOS launchd, Linux cron. Pings fire even if your terminal is closed.

Fully Configurable

Tune burn rate, slot duration, ping message, working directory, and more. Everything stored in a single JSON config file.

Interactive Setup

Run claude-shift init and answer a few questions. It configures everything and installs the schedule for you.

Zero Daemon

No background process eating memory. Your OS’s native scheduler fires pings at the right time. Install once, runs forever.
