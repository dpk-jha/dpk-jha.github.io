AutoDeck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026  ·  v1.7.1

No personal data collected

Overview

AutoDeck (com.dpkay.apps.autodeck) is an Android app that lets you create named app lists ("profiles", also called "drawers") and automatically switch between them based on triggers such as Bluetooth connections, Wi-Fi networks, location, time schedules, phone and internet calls, turn-by-turn navigation, and NFC tags.

This policy describes how Deepak Jha ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with the App. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics — everything you create stays on your device.

The App does use a small number of Google services that are part of the Android platform and the Google Play ecosystem: Google Play services Location (geofencing), the Google Maps SDK (only inside the map picker used to choose a location trigger), and Google Play Billing (only if you take the optional Pro subscription). These are described in the Third-Party Services section below.

What the App Does

All drawer and trigger processing happens entirely on your device. The App provides the following features:

App Drawers
Create named lists of apps ("drawers" or "profiles") and switch between them with a tap or automatically.
Home Screen Widget
A Glance-based widget that shows the active drawer and lets you switch drawers directly from your home screen.
Bluetooth Triggers
Automatically activate a drawer when a specific Bluetooth device connects or disconnects.
Wi-Fi Triggers
Switch drawers when you connect to or disconnect from a specific Wi-Fi network.
Location Triggers
Activate a drawer when you enter or exit a geographic area (geofence).
Schedule Triggers
Set time-based rules to switch drawers at specific times or on specific days.
Call Triggers
Switch drawers when a cellular or internet (VoIP) call starts or ends.
Navigation Triggers
Switch drawers when turn-by-turn navigation starts or ends in a maps app.
NFC Triggers
Tap an NFC tag to activate a specific drawer.
Media Triggers
Switch drawers when music, a podcast, or a video starts or stops playing.
Flight Mode Triggers
Switch drawers when Airplane Mode is turned on or off.
Charging Triggers
Switch drawers when the device starts or stops charging.
Do Not Disturb Triggers
Switch drawers when Do Not Disturb is turned on or off.
Wired Audio Triggers
Switch drawers when wired earphones, speakers, or other wired audio are connected or disconnected.
Multi-Condition Rules
Combine multiple trigger conditions (AND/OR) to create complex automation rules.
Static Drawer Widget
Pin a specific drawer to your home screen as its own widget, independent of the active drawer.
Widget Grid Customisation
Adjust widget grid size, spacing, distribution, labels, and appearance with a live preview.
Favorites (Sticky Apps)
Choose apps and shortcuts that appear in every drawer, before or after the drawer's own apps.
Shortcuts & Deep Links
Activate drawers from launcher shortcuts, autodeck:// deep links, or broadcasts sent by automation apps such as Samsung Modes & Routines, Tasker, and MacroDroid.
Activity Log
An on-device log of drawer switches and trigger events that you can review and share for troubleshooting.
Languages
The App interface is available in 18 languages, selectable per-app in Android's language settings.
Theme
Choose whether the App follows the system light/dark setting, or stays light or dark.
“More apps” View
Pick how extra apps open from the widget: a bottom sheet, a launcher-style folder, or a full screen.
Keep Alive
An optional, off-by-default service that keeps AutoDeck resident so widget taps and automation commands respond without a cold start.
Pro Upgrade
An optional monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks the Location (geofence) trigger, handled by Google Play Billing.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store:

Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
Device identifiers or advertising IDs
Usage analytics or crash reports sent to our servers
Call logs, contact lists, or message content
Notification content from other apps (see BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE below for the narrow, in-memory exception used to detect navigation, VoIP calls, and media session state)
Browsing history or network traffic
Location history (geofence events are processed on-device and not stored beyond the active session)

Permissions Used

The App requests the following Android permissions solely to perform its core functions. Each permission is tied to a specific trigger type and is only used when you enable that trigger:

BLUETOOTH · BLUETOOTH_CONNECT
Used by Bluetooth triggers to detect when paired Bluetooth devices connect or disconnect. The App reads device names to let you select which device to use as a trigger. No Bluetooth data is transmitted off your device.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION · ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION · ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION
Used by location (geofence) triggers to detect when you enter or exit a geographic area. Location is processed on-device by Android's geofencing APIs. The App does not store location history or transmit your location to any server. Background location access is required so geofence triggers can fire when the App is not in the foreground.
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE
Used by Wi-Fi triggers to detect which Wi-Fi network you are connected to. The App reads the network name (SSID) to match against your configured triggers. No Wi-Fi data is transmitted off your device.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Used alongside the Wi-Fi permission to tell whether the device is currently on a Wi-Fi network at all, so a Wi-Fi trigger can be re-checked after the App restarts. Only the connected / not-connected state is read, and none of it leaves your device.
READ_PHONE_STATE
Used by call triggers to detect when a phone call starts or ends. The App does not read phone numbers, call logs, or any call content — it only observes the call state (ringing, off-hook, idle).
NFC
Used by NFC triggers to detect when an NFC tag is tapped. The App reads the tag ID to match against your configured triggers. No NFC data is transmitted off your device.
BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE
Used by the navigation, internet-call, and media triggers. For every notification posted on the device the App looks at two things only — the source package name and the notification's category — to decide whether it is navigation or an internet call; for any other app it stops there. It looks further only for a fixed list of apps: Google Maps and Waze for navigation, and WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet for internet calls. For those it additionally reads, in memory and on your device only, the notification channel, the ongoing flag, and — for Google Maps alone — the title text, which it matches against a fixed distance pattern such as “400 m”. The media trigger uses this same permission differently: it is not notification parsing, but authorization to call Android's MediaSessionManager, which reports only whether a media session exists and whether it is currently playing — never the track title, artist, or artwork. No notification or media content is stored, logged, transmitted off your device, sold, or shared. This service must be enabled manually by you in Android Settings and can be revoked at any time.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
Used by schedule triggers to fire drawer switches at specific times. Alarms are managed by Android's AlarmManager and processed entirely on-device.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Used to re-register triggers (geofences, alarms, Bluetooth/Wi-Fi monitors) after the device restarts, so your automation rules continue to work without manually reopening the App.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE · FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION
Used to run a foreground service for Wi-Fi monitoring and location-based geofence triggers. A visible notification is shown while the service is active, as required by Android.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE
Used only by the optional Keep alive setting (Settings › System, off by default). When you turn it on, the App runs an empty foreground service with a permanent low-priority notification so the system keeps AutoDeck resident, and widget taps and automation commands do not have to cold-start it. The service reads no data of any kind and does nothing but exist; turning the setting off stops it immediately.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS
Declared only so Settings › System › Battery optimization can show Android's one-tap exemption dialog instead of dropping you into the system-wide battery list. Triggers are long-running background listeners, and an optimised process stops receiving them. Granting the exemption is entirely your choice, it is never requested silently, and it can be revoked from Android Settings at any time.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Used to show notifications for foreground services and trigger activity. No marketing or promotional notifications are sent.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES
Used to list installed apps so you can select which apps to include in a drawer. The list of installed apps is never transmitted off your device.
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS
Optional, and only if you turn on Recent apps. Used to see which apps you opened in the last few minutes so they can be shown on your widget. This is read on your device each time the widget updates, is never stored by the App, and is never transmitted off your device. You can revoke it at any time in Android Settings → Usage access.
INTERNET
Required by the Google libraries the App uses: the Google Maps SDK (to load map tiles in the location-trigger map picker) and Google Play Billing (to process the optional Pro purchase). We operate no servers, and the App never sends your drawers, rules, app list, or logs anywhere.

Local Data & Storage

The App stores data only on your device using Android Room (SQLite) and DataStore. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App. The following information is stored locally:

Drawers & app lists — the names and contents of drawers you create, and which drawer is currently active.
Trigger rules — your configured automation rules (Bluetooth device names, Wi-Fi SSIDs, geofence coordinates, schedule times, etc.).
Wi-Fi network history — the names (SSIDs) of Wi-Fi networks this device connected to while a Wi-Fi trigger was active, each with the time it was last seen. This exists only so the Wi-Fi trigger picker can offer you networks you have already used instead of an empty list. It stays on your device and is never transmitted; you can erase it at any time from Android Settings › Apps › AutoDeck › Storage › Clear data, or by uninstalling the App.
Trigger session state — which triggers are currently active, used to evaluate multi-condition rules. Cleaned up automatically.
Favorites (sticky apps) — the apps and shortcuts you pin to every drawer, and their position.
Recent apps settings — whether Recent apps is on, how many to show, the time window, their position, and the list of apps you chose to hide from it.
UI & widget preferences — active drawer selection, widget grid size, spacing, label and appearance settings, your theme choice (system / light / dark), the “More apps” view style, the keep-alive on/off flag, and other user preferences via DataStore.
Setup state — whether you have finished onboarding, and which permission disclosures you have already accepted, so you are not shown them again.
Activity log — a plain-text log of drawer switches and trigger events (see Activity Log & Diagnostics), kept in the App's private storage and deleted automatically after 7 days.
Pro subscription status — a single true/false flag caching whether an active Pro subscription was reported by Google Play. No payment details are stored by the App.

Because we do not collect any personal data, there is nothing on our servers to retain or delete on your behalf.

Activity Log & Diagnostics

To help you understand and troubleshoot your automation rules, the App keeps a plain-text activity log in its private storage. Each entry is a timestamp, a category, and a short line such as which drawer was activated, which rule fired, or which Bluetooth device connected. Log files rotate weekly and are deleted automatically after 7 days.

The log records only events the App itself handles, for example:

Drawer activations, deactivations and restores, by drawer name
Rule and trigger evaluation results, by rule label
Bluetooth device name and address, Wi-Fi network name (SSID), geofence entry/exit, alarm start/end, NFC tag scans
Whether a cellular or internet call started or ended, and whether navigation started or ended and in which app (Google Maps or Waze) — never phone numbers, contacts, or call or notification content
App lifecycle lines such as the keep-alive service starting or stopping

Sharing is always your choice. Logs never leave your device unless you tap Share Logs in Settings. When you do, the App assembles the stored logs together with a diagnostic header — app version, device manufacturer and model, Android version, your default launcher, and which permissions are granted or denied — into a single text file and hands it to the app you pick in the Android share sheet. Nothing is uploaded to us, and we receive it only if you deliberately send it to us.

Purchases

The App offers an optional Pro subscription (monthly or yearly) that unlocks the Location (geofence) trigger. All other features are free. Subscriptions are sold, billed, renewed, and cancelled entirely through Google Play Billing and your Google Play account. We never see or handle your payment method, billing address, or Google account details — the App only receives from Google Play whether an active subscription exists, and stores that as a single true/false flag on your device.

Google's handling of purchase data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The App contains no analytics libraries, no advertising networks, and no crash reporting tools. It does rely on three Google libraries, each limited to a single feature:

Google Play services — Location
Provides Android's geofencing APIs used by Location triggers. Geofences are registered with Google Play services on your device, which notifies the App on entry or exit. The App does not send your location anywhere and does not store location history.
Google Maps SDK for Android
Used only in the map picker you open when creating a location trigger, so you can see and drop a pin on a map. Displaying the map requires Google to serve map tiles, so opening the picker contacts Google's servers. If you never create a location trigger, no map is ever loaded.
Google Play Billing
Used only to offer and verify the optional Pro subscription. See Purchases above.

Data handled by these Google services is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Everything else in the App is implemented using Android platform APIs.
