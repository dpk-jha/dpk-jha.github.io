Why it exists

Your phone does different things at different times. Your home screen should too.

A normal home screen shows the same apps all day. So you hunt for maps while driving, scroll past work chat on a Sunday, and keep rearranging folders that were right yesterday.

How it works

Three pieces. Then it runs itself.

  1. Make a profile

    A profile is a named set of apps: Work, Driving, Gym, Weekend. Pick the apps and the order.

  2. Add a rule

    A rule says when to switch: “When I connect to My Car, switch to Driving.” When you disconnect, it can switch back by itself.

  3. Place the widget

    One widget on your home screen shows the apps of whichever profile is on. That’s the only thing that changes.

Turn on a small dot on the widget. Tap it, choose another profile, and its apps join the widget. Tap the dot again and they leave. You don’t change profile and nothing else is interrupted. It’s the easy way to grab one app from another part of your day.

Privacy

Your places and networks stay on your phone.

AutoDeck needs to know where you are and what you’re connected to in order to do its job. It doesn’t need to tell anyone.

Kept on your phone

Your location is checked against your own places on your phone. Wi-Fi names, Bluetooth devices and schedules work the same way.

No account, no tracking

No sign-up, no servers of ours, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools inside the app.

Asked for, one at a time

A permission is requested only when you pick the trigger that needs it. Skip location and everything else still works.

Questions

Good questions.

Does it work with my launcher?

AutoDeck is a normal home screen widget, so it works with any launcher that supports widgets, such as Samsung One UI, Pixel Launcher, Nova, Lawnchair, Microsoft Launcher and Action Launcher. You keep the launcher you already use.

Is AutoDeck a launcher?

No. It’s an app plus a home screen widget. The widget is the part that changes as your profile changes.

Can I use my own icon pack?

Yes. Pick an installed icon pack for every profile, or give one profile its own. Packs made for Nova, ADW and GO launchers are supported.

Why does a Wi-Fi rule ask for location?

Android treats the name of the Wi-Fi network you are connected to as location information, so an app can only read it with the location permission. AutoDeck reads the name when you connect, not all the time, and it does not read your position for a Wi-Fi rule.

Does it need an account or the internet?

There is no account. Rules run on your phone. The internet is used by the map where you pick a place, and by Google Play when you buy Pro.

What is free and what is Pro?

Every trigger is free, and so is every customisation. The free plan runs 2 rules; Pro removes that limit and lets a rule combine conditions. Switching profiles from Tasker, MacroDroid, Samsung Routines or a shortcut is free too.

Which Android versions and languages?

Android 8.0 or newer. The app is available in 18 languages: English, Arabic, Chinese, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Greek, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Persian, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Thai and Turkish.
